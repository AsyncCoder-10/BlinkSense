Afternoon drowsiness: take a 10-minute walk or short break and avoid heavy meals before studying.
Pomodoro: 25 minutes focus, 5 minutes break; after 4 cycles take a 20-minute break.
Frequent looking away: keep your phone out of sight, close extra tabs, use a website blocker.
Low blink rate and tired eyes: follow the 20-20-20 rule, every 20 minutes look 20 feet away for 20 seconds.
Drowsy while studying: drink water, improve room lighting, and study standing for a few minutes.
Short focus streaks: start with 15-minute sessions and increase gradually.
Poor sleep reduces attention: aim for consistent 7-8 hours and avoid screens before bed.
Distraction from noise: use noise-cancelling headphones or low-volume instrumental music.
